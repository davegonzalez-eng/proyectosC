Option B prototype: 5-arm spiral star

A single face rendered in isolation: 5 rotationally-symmetric logarithmic-spiral arms (one per pentagon vertex), each narrow at its outward tip and widening continuously toward the center, where all 5 overlap into a solid hub with no gap - like a real 5-pointed star, not a pinwheel of paddles. Not wired into the full dodecahedron yet.

Note: "Hole loop turns" doesn't currently close a terminal eye-hole - since the band is now widest at the hub, a loop past that point continues at full width instead of narrowing into an open ring. Revisit this if/when the eye-hole is reintroduced.
