Dodecahedron Star Generator

A Quin-style study: swirled & waving stars on each pentagonal face, connected by ribbons.

Longer arms (reach past 1.0) plus a spiral concentrated near the tip - together with tip dip below, this gets the arm's own trajectory most of the way to where its ribbon needs to go, so the ribbon only has to finish a curve that's already underway instead of u-turning from a standing start.
Extra inward pull toward the sculpture's center, concentrated right at each arm's tip - the arm curls inward before ending, so the ribbon it turns into continues an already-underway curve instead of starting flat.
Lowest point of a ribbon, as % of the face's own radius from the sculpture's center (80% = ducks a bit under the surface).
Always on. For each arm, follows the edge to the neighboring face and lands on its matching arm there (the pattern behind F6-A0:F7-A2, F1-A2:F7-A1, etc.), applied identically to every face - every arm ends up connected.
