Dodecahedron Star Generator

A Quin-style study: swirled & waving stars on each pentagonal face, connected by ribbons.

For each arm, follows the edge to the neighboring face and lands on its matching arm there (the pattern behind F6-A0:F7-A2 etc.) — 60 ribbons, every arm touched twice. Passes under the stars, at a lower radius than the surface.
List arm pairs to connect, e.g. F0-A2:F5-A1, F2-A3:F9-A0. Or just click two arm markers in the 3D view to add a pair automatically.
