Extra inward pull toward the sculpture's center, concentrated right at each arm's tip - the arm curls inward before ending, so the ribbon it turns into continues an already-underway curve instead of starting flat.
Lowest point of a ribbon, as % of the face's own radius from the sculpture's center (93% = stays just under the surface).

Real slab thickness (world units) of the ribbons and of the star's interior fill - equal defaults so both read as the same sheet stock. Ribbon ends still taper to the tube's own flattened thickness at the joins.
Jitters the cell lattice and varies strut widths so the perforations read organic/cellular - coral, bone, a Voronoi diagram - instead of a mechanical hex grid. 0 = perfectly regular hexagons.
